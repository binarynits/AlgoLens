AlgoLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version

AlgoLens collects no personal information. The app runs entirely on your device, makes no network requests, and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your lesson completion progress and session XP/streak are stored locally using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data never leaves your device. It is included in your iCloud device backup only if you have iCloud Backup enabled in iOS Settings, in which case Apple's own privacy policy applies to that backup.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not show advertisements.
  • We do not require an account or any sign-in.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
  • We do not make any network requests from the app.

Children's privacy

AlgoLens does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app does not transmit any data, it is safe to use by learners of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of AlgoLens adds any feature that collects or transmits data (for example, an optional cloud sync for progress), this page will be updated before that version ships and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the project's GitHub repository: github.com/binarynits/AlgoLens
